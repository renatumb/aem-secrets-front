AEM Secrets ("we", "us") operates the website at aemsecrets.com. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Newsletter subscription: name and email address when you use the subscription form.

  • Comments: the name and comment text you submit on posts (email is not shown publicly).

  • Analytics: we use Google Analytics 4 to measure visits, page views, and approximate location (country/region). Google may set cookies or similar identifiers on your device. Analytics runs only after you accept optional cookies.

How we use your information

  • Send occasional newsletter emails with AEM tips and blog updates (only if you subscribed).
  • Respond to contact form messages.
  • Display and moderate public comments.
  • Operate and secure the author dashboard.
  • Measure site usage with Google Analytics (with your consent).

Legal basis (EEA/UK visitors)

We rely on your consent for newsletter emails and for Google Analytics cookies, on our legitimate interest in replying to contact messages and operating the site, and on contractual necessity where needed to provide services you request.

Retention

We keep subscriber and contact details for as long as needed to provide the service or until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data. Comment data may remain visible until moderated or removed.

Cookies and Google Analytics

Strictly necessary storage (theme preference) stays in your browser's local storage and is not used for advertising. Optional Google Analytics cookies are used only if you click Accept on the cookie banner.

If you decline, we tell Google (Consent Mode) not to store analytics cookies. You can also use browser controls or Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

Unsubscribe from emails

Every marketing email includes a one-click unsubscribe link with a unique token. That link opens our unsubscribe page, which deactivates your subscription without requiring a login. You can also contact us and ask to be removed.

Sharing and processors

We do not sell your personal data. Data is processed on our hosting infrastructure, email delivery providers, and Google (as analytics processor) when you have accepted analytics cookies. We do not use Google Analytics for advertising.
