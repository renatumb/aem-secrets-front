AEM Secrets ("we", "us") operates the website at aemsecrets.com. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Newsletter subscription: name and email address when you use the subscription form.

  • Comments: the name and comment text you submit on posts (email is not shown publicly).

How we use your information

  • Send occasional newsletter emails with AEM tips and blog updates (only if you subscribed).
  • Respond to contact form messages.
  • Display and moderate public comments.
  • Protect public forms from spam and abuse with Google reCAPTCHA.

Legal basis (EEA/UK visitors)

We rely on your consent for newsletter emails, on our legitimate interest in replying to contact messages, operating the site, and securing public forms against spam (including Google reCAPTCHA), and on contractual necessity where needed to provide services you request.

Retention

We keep subscriber and contact details for as long as needed to provide the service or until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data. Comment data may remain visible until moderated or removed.

Cookies and local storage

Strictly necessary storage (theme preference and, for authors, a session token) stays in your browser's local storage and is not used for advertising. Google reCAPTCHA may set cookies or similar identifiers when you use a protected form; that is required for spam protection.

Unsubscribe from emails

Every marketing email includes a one-click unsubscribe link with a unique token. That link opens our unsubscribe page, which deactivates your subscription without requiring a login. You can also contact us and ask to be removed.

Sharing and processors

We do not sell your personal data. Data is processed on our hosting infrastructure, email delivery providers, and Google — as reCAPTCHA processor for abuse prevention on public forms. Google's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service also apply to reCAPTCHA.

Your rights
